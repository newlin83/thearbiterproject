Expression of Intent
to Enroll

This document initiates the ninety-day Expression of Intent period. It is not an application and creates no legal obligation on either side. You are simply beginning.

Before You Begin

The Expression of Intent is the first step in the Arbitration enrollment process. Upon submission, you will be assigned an Enrollment Advisor who will contact you within seven business days to schedule your initial orientation session.

Your Enrollment Advisor is not a salesperson. Their certified role is to ensure you have complete and accurate information about every aspect of what you are considering. They are prohibited from encouraging enrollment and required to present benefits, requirements, and constraints with equal weight.

You may withdraw this Expression of Intent at any time, without penalty, without explanation, and without any effect on your civil standing. Nothing becomes binding until all seventeen agreements have been signed and confirmed by the relevant federal and state district courts — a process that takes a minimum of seven months from first signature.

Section IV
Questions and Concerns
Your Enrollment Advisor will address these directly in your first orientation session. No concern is too small, too obvious, or too specific to be worth recording here.
Section V
Attestation

By submitting this Expression of Intent, I attest that the information provided in this document is accurate to the best of my knowledge, that I am submitting this document voluntarily and without inducement, and that I understand this submission creates no legal obligation on my part or on the part of the Civic Arbitration System.

I understand that I will be assigned an Enrollment Advisor within seven business days and that I am under no obligation to proceed beyond this initial step. I understand that enrollment is a voluntary process and that I may withdraw at any time prior to the completion of all seventeen agreements and their judicial confirmation, without penalty and without explanation.

I understand that the permanent delegation of my civic voting franchise, if I choose to proceed to full enrollment, constitutes a legally binding and irrevocable act under the terms of the Twenty-Eighth Amendment and the Civic Delegation Act as amended, and that this Expression of Intent is not that act.

Upon submission, a case number will be generated and assigned to your file. Your Enrollment Advisor will contact you using the email address provided within seven business days to schedule your initial ninety-minute orientation session.
